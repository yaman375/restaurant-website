02 — OUR STORY
HOW IT BEGAN2014 — TODAY

Rooted here.
Open to everywhere.

NOIR began with a small room, a few market crates and a belief that a great meal should feel personal. A decade on, that belief still leads us: buy thoughtfully, cook with curiosity, and make every guest feel expected.

Our kitchen follows what is growing, swimming and tasting its best. Our dining room follows your lead. Whether it is a long-celebrated occasion or simply a Tuesday worth remembering, there is a place for you at our table.
